Our retention of personal data

We hold on to your personal data for as long as it’s necessary to deliver the products and carry out the transactions you’ve requested, or for other valid reasons such as legal compliance, dispute resolution, and enforcement of our agreements. The retention periods can significantly differ, depending on various factors, including the data type, our interactions with you, and your use of our products. Here are some of the factors we consider when deciding on appropriate retention periods:

  1. User expectations: if you provide, create, or maintain data with the expectation that we will keep it until you choose to remove it, we respect that expectation. For instance, if you create a post or a reply on FydeOS Community, we aim to keep that data until you actively decide to delete it.
  2. Sensitivity of data: if the personal data is sensitive in nature, we generally adopt a shorter retention period.
  3. User consent: if you have given your consent for a longer retention period, we will keep your data according to your consent.
